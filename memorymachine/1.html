one of my biggest regrets is not taking pictures of guangzhou before leaving it for canada. I haven’t been in guangzhou in maybe. Seven? Years? I’ve always had this or that to do. sports camps. Portfolio prep. SATs. Trying to find a job. my parents still go back every year. I’m not sure the guangzhou my parents see, refreshed over and over again in their memory, is the one I left behind. A city doesn’t have legs or a body but it’s nonetheless left me behind, as if I hadn’t been raised under its sky for eleven years and then some. I miss it but I have nothing left of it in my hands. Images are a poor substitute for standing beneath its sky but I think I’d take an iCloud album overflowing with shitty 2010s cameraphone pictures just the same. In some weird perversion of theseus’ ship I’d like to ask: has a ship ever existed if it was yours and yours alone and for a decade you keep hacking and shaving and whittling it down to its wooden bones and then keep going until it was a mere vessel of air?
