In this magical age of technology we tend to think it can patch up any of our needs or fuckups or regrets. Here’s a continuation of that pesky little theseus’ ship problem—if google lens takes a poor screenshot of one tree and spits back out fifty similar trees, are any of these trees really distinguishable? Can new planks of wood substitute old wood? To what extent is my memory mutable, exchangeable? does it really matter what tree, if I can’t remember which tree at all? I find the trees under my apartment building in India, Thailand, anywhere that, according to the Wikipedia article that popped up, is subtropical. I still don’t remember the species name. I find the same kind of sunlight filtering the same kind of blurry leaves in Korea. I find familiar bricks from real estate in Hong Kong. I find no parking signs in Germany and overcast skies in NBCnews and white Lexuses from wherever they have dealerships to sell white Lexuses. i rebuild my ship anew through shutterstock and YouTube shorts and instagram reels and reddit threads and flickr albums and LinkedIn profiles and Facebook groups and TripAdvisor hotels and Amazon listings. i search and I search… ad nauseam.
